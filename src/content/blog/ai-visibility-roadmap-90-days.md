The Arrow method fits in four words: **listen, decode, amplify, measure.** This roadmap turns them into 13 weeks of concrete work. It assumes one owner with a few hours a week and access to your website, and it works whether you do it yourself or with a partner.

## Phase 1 (weeks 1–2): listen and build the baseline

**Goal:** a dated, evidence-based picture of how AI engines describe you today.

- **Week 1: Questions and competitors.** Write 20–50 real buyer questions grouped by intent (category, problem, comparison, alternatives, brand facts). Define 3–8 competitors. Freeze both.
- **Week 1: Protocol.** Pick 2–4 engines; fix session, location, language and mode.
- **Week 2: Run and record.** Run every question, save full answers, dates and cited URLs. Score mentions, own-URL citations, recommendations and accuracy.
- **Week 2: Readout.** Compute rates and [share of voice](/blog/ai-share-of-voice/). List the top cited domains for your category.

**Deliverable:** baseline report + list of gaps. Method: [how to measure AI visibility](/blog/how-to-measure-ai-visibility/).

## Phase 2 (weeks 3–4): decode and remove the blockers

**Goal:** make sure nothing technical or factual stops engines from using you.

- **Week 3: Access.** robots.txt, CDN/WAF bot rules and server logs for OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot and Bingbot; indexing in Google and Bing; key text in raw HTML; sitemaps. → [Technical checklist](/blog/technical-ai-visibility-checklist/)
- **Week 3: Accuracy triage.** For every wrong fact in the baseline, find its source and fix it.
- **Week 4: Entity.** Write your canonical description; align homepage, About, footer, Organization schema with `sameAs`, and your main profiles and directories. → [Entity clarity](/blog/entity-clarity-ai-search/)

**Deliverable:** blockers fixed, one consistent description of the business everywhere.

## Phase 3 (weeks 5–10): amplify and strengthen the source

**Goal:** give engines better material to cite and better reasons to recommend you.

- **Weeks 5–6: Prioritise.** From the baseline, pick the 5–8 questions with the highest business value where you’re absent or not recommended. For each, note what the engine cites instead.
- **Weeks 6–9: Answer pages.** Publish or rewrite one page per priority question: answer first, self-contained sections, specifics, evidence, honest limits, FAQ. Typical candidates: pricing, “is it right for…”, comparisons, use-case pages. → [Content AI engines cite](/blog/content-ai-engines-cite/)
- **Weeks 6–9: Structured data.** Article, Product/Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList that match each page. → [Structured data](/blog/schema-markup-ai-visibility/)
- **Weeks 7–10: Evidence.** Work the top cited domains: correct listings, request reviews from real customers, pitch data or expertise to the publications that appear in your answers. → [Third-party sources](/blog/third-party-sources-ai-trust/)
- **Week 8: Analytics.** Set up the AI assistants channel in GA4 and a “How did you find us?” field. → [AI referral traffic](/blog/track-ai-referral-traffic-ga4/)

**Deliverable:** 5–8 answer pages live, schema aligned, evidence work underway, tracking in place.

## Phase 4 (weeks 11–13): measure and re-read the signal

**Goal:** an honest before/after on the same panel.

- **Week 11: Re-run.** Same questions, same engines, same protocol.
- **Week 12: Compare.** Rates and share of voice vs. baseline, per engine and intent; citations of the new pages; accuracy; AI referral sessions and leads.
- **Week 13: Report and plan.** One page: what moved, what didn’t, why, and the next 5–8 questions to work on.

**Deliverable:** before/after report backed by saved answers, and the next cycle’s plan.

## What “good” looks like after 90 days

Every category is different, so set targets against your own baseline:

- blockers found in phase 2 are **fixed**;
- **wrong facts** found in the baseline are corrected at the source;
- new answer pages are **indexed** and start appearing as **citations** on the questions they target;
- **recommendation share** moves on at least some priority questions;
- AI referral traffic is **tracked**, even if small.
